🏓 Ping Pong Challenge

Ping Pong Challenge is a modern twist on the classic Pong game.
The rules are simple, but the difficulty increases over time:

⏳ As time passes, your paddle (bar) gradually shrinks, making it harder to defend.

⚡ The ball’s speed increases as the game continues, testing your reflexes.

🏆 Scoring:

Normal hit = +1 point

Full-speed hit = +2 points (extra reward for surviving at high speed)
